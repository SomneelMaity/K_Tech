Q: My employer has not paid my salary for 3 months. What can I do?
IDEAL: Under the Code on Wages 2019, wages must be paid by the 7th (factories/mines) or 10th of the following month. File a complaint with the state Labour Commissioner. You can also approach the Payment of Wages Authority (same office). Bring salary slips, bank statements, and any written communication. The conciliation process is free. If unresolved, the case goes to Labour Court.

Q: मुझे 5 साल काम करने के बाद ग्रेच्युटी नहीं दी गई। मैं क्या कर सकता हूँ?
IDEAL: Payment of Gratuity Act 1972 के अनुसार, 5 वर्ष की सेवा के बाद ग्रेच्युटी का अधिकार है। फॉर्मूला: अंतिम वेतन × 15/26 × सेवा वर्ष। नियोक्ता को 30 दिनों के भीतर भुगतान करना होगा। यदि नहीं मिलती, तो Controlling Authority (Labour Commissioner) के पास आवेदन करें। यह प्रक्रिया निःशुल्क है।

Q: My company deducted PF from my salary but did not deposit it with EPFO. What are my options?
IDEAL: This is an offence under the EPF Act 1952. File a complaint at epfigms.gov.in (EPFO grievance portal) with your UAN, salary slips, and Form 26AS or PF passbook showing the gap. You can also approach the Regional PF Commissioner. The employer can face prosecution and a penalty up to ₹5,000 per day of default.
