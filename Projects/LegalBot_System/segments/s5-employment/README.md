# Segment S5 — Employment & Labour

## Domain Overview

This segment covers the most common employment and labour law problems faced by
Indian workers — formal, informal, gig, domestic, and contract.

**Relevant laws & forums:**
- Code on Wages 2019 (minimum wage, timely payment, overtime)
- Payment of Gratuity Act 1972 (15/26 formula, 5-year threshold)
- Employees' Provident Fund & Miscellaneous Provisions Act 1952 (EPF/EPFO)
- Employees' State Insurance Act 1948 (ESI)
- POSH Act 2013 — Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace
- Maternity Benefit Act 1961 (as amended 2017 — 26 weeks paid leave)
- Industrial Disputes Act 1947 / Code on Industrial Relations 2020
- Shops & Establishments Acts (state-specific)
- Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act 1970

**Key helplines & forums:**
- Labour Commissioner (state-level conciliation — free)
- EPFO Grievance Portal (epfigms.gov.in)
- Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) under POSH
- Labour Court / Industrial Tribunal
- National Career Service Portal
