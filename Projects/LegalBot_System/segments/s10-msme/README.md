# Segment S10 — MSME & Small Business

## Domain Overview

This segment covers the most common legal and compliance problems faced by
micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) and small-business founders in India.

**Relevant laws & forums:**
- MSMED Act 2006 — 45-day payment rule, 3× bank-rate compound interest,
  MSME Samadhaan portal for delayed payments
- Negotiable Instruments Act 1881 §138 — cheque bounce (demand notice within 30 days)
- Indian Contract Act 1872 — contract enforceability, limitation
- Goods & Services Tax (GST) — registration, filing basics
- Shops & Establishments Acts (state-specific — licence requirement)
- FSSAI Act 2006 — food business licence
- Startup India / DPIIT recognition
- Company / LLP / Partnership registration (MCA portal)

**Key portals & forums:**
- MSME Samadhaan (samadhaan.msme.gov.in) — delayed payment portal
- MSME Facilitation Council (state-level arbitration for §18 disputes)
- National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) for insolvency
- Consumer Forum for B2C disputes
