Q: My client owes me ₹80,000 for 5 months and is not paying. I am an MSME. What can I do?
IDEAL: Under MSMED Act 2006 Section 15–16, if a buyer has not paid within 45 days of acceptance of goods/services, they owe compound interest at 3× the RBI bank rate from the due date. File on the MSME Samadhaan portal (samadhaan.msme.gov.in) — it is free and the MSME Facilitation Council will conciliate. Ensure you have Udyam registration. Keep invoice copies, delivery proof, and any written communication.

Q: A customer gave me a cheque for ₹50,000 that bounced. What should I do and how fast?
IDEAL: Under Negotiable Instruments Act 1881 Section 138, you must send a written legal notice within 30 days of receiving the bank's dishonour memo. The notice demands payment within 15 days. If unpaid, file a criminal complaint in the Magistrate's court within 30 days after the 15-day period ends. This is a strict timeline — missing it bars you from criminal action. Keep the original cheque, bank memo, and proof of notice delivery.

PERSONA: Arjun
VERIFIED: pending
