## What is Automatically Protected by Copyright?

Copyright arises **automatically** on creation of original work — no registration
required. It protects:
- Written content (website text, manuals, blogs, brochures)
- Software code (Section 2(ffc) — treated as "literary work")
- Photographs and videos
- Artistic works (logos, illustrations, packaging design)
- Music and audio recordings

## Duration

- Works by individuals: Author's life + **60 years** after death
- Corporate works (anonymous, pseudonymous, published by company): **60 years** from
  publication

## Why Register? (Voluntary)

While not required, registration with the **Copyright Office** provides:
- **Prima facie evidence** of ownership in court disputes
- Easier enforcement in customs (blocking infringing imports)
- Fee: ₹500 per work (online at copyright.gov.in)

## Who Owns Copyright in a Business Context?

- Work created by an **employee in the course of employment** → employer owns
  copyright (Section 17), unless the contract says otherwise
- Work by a **freelancer/contractor** → contractor owns copyright unless explicitly
  assigned in writing

**Always include a copyright assignment clause in freelancer contracts.**

## Common Infringements for MSMEs

- Competitor copying your website content, product photos, or software
- Using stock images without licence
- Copying your logo (this is also trademark infringement)

## Enforcement

1. Send a **Cease and Desist** notice to the infringer
2. File **FIR** under Section 63 (criminal) — infringement is cognizable
3. Civil suit for injunction + damages (Section 55)
4. For online infringement: DMCA/Copyright takedown to Google, social media platforms
