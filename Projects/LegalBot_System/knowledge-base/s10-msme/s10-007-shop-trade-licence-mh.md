## Two Separate Licences Required in Maharashtra

### 1. Shops & Establishments Registration (State)

- Mandatory for every shop, commercial establishment, restaurant, or office in
  Maharashtra with **at least 1 employee**
- **Online registration** at aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in
- Issued by the **Labour Commissioner, Maharashtra**
- Certificate valid for **5 years**, renewable online
- Fee: approximately ₹100–₹2,000 depending on number of employees

Documents needed:
- Business address proof (electricity bill / lease deed)
- PAN and Aadhaar of proprietor
- Details of employees

### 2. Trade Licence (Local Municipal Body)

- Required from the **local municipal corporation** (MCGM in Mumbai, PMC in Pune, etc.)
- Renewed **annually**
- Issued under the Maharashtra Municipal Corporations Act / Nagar Panchayat rules

**Mumbai (MCGM)**: Apply at **mcgm.gov.in** or nearest Ward Office  
**Pune (PMC)**: Apply at **pmc.gov.in**

Documents typically needed:
- Ownership/tenancy proof of premises
- NOC from landlord (if rented)
- Site plan / premises map
- PAN, Aadhaar, passport photo of owner

## Food Business — Additional Licence

If the shop involves food preparation or sale, a **FSSAI licence** is also required
(see entry s10-008).

## Penalty for Operating Without Licence

- Shops & Establishments: Fine up to ₹10,000 (first offence), ₹20,000 (subsequent)
- Trade licence: Municipal fine and potential sealing of premises
