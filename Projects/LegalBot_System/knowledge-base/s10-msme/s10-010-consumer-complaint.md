## Who Can File a Consumer Complaint?

Any **consumer** — a person who buys goods or avails services for personal use
(not for commercial resale) — can file a complaint for:
- **Defective goods** (manufacturing defect, quality issues)
- **Deficiency in services** (incomplete service, delay, negligence)
- **Unfair trade practices** (misleading advertisements, overcharging)
- **Restrictive trade practices** or denial of warranty

## Jurisdiction Based on Claim Value

| Forum | Claim Value |
|-------|------------|
| **District Consumer Commission** | Up to ₹50 lakh |
| **State Consumer Commission** | ₹50 lakh – ₹2 crore |
| **National Consumer Commission (NCDRC)** | Above ₹2 crore |

## How to File (Online — Free)

1. Visit **consumerhelpline.gov.in** or **edaakhil.nic.in**
2. Register and fill complaint details (opponent's name, address, facts, relief sought)
3. Attach supporting documents (invoice, warranty card, photos, correspondence)
4. Pay nominal court fee (₹0–₹5,000 depending on claim value)

## Timeline

- Commission must **admit or reject** within 21 days of receiving the complaint
- Opposite party must respond within **30 days**
- Target decision: within **3–5 months** (simple cases)

## Available Reliefs

- Replacement or repair of defective goods
- Full refund with interest
- Compensation for mental agony and inconvenience
- Punitive damages for willful neglect

## National Consumer Helpline

- Call **1800-11-4000** (toll-free) — for pre-complaint mediation with the company
- Registered companies often resolve complaints via the helpline to avoid forum proceedings
- Also available via WhatsApp: **8800001915**
