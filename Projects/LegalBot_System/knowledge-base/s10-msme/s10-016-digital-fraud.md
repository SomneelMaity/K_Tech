## Types of Digital Payment Fraud Common to MSMEs

- **UPI / bank account fraud**: Fraudster impersonates a buyer and sends a
  collect request instead of a payment request
- **Phishing**: Fake bank/GST portals stealing login credentials
- **OTP fraud**: Caller poses as bank/GSTIN officer requesting OTP
- **Fake payment screenshots**: Buyer sends a forged UPI success screenshot
- **Business email compromise (BEC)**: Hacker intercepts email and changes your
  bank account details in invoices

## Immediate Steps When Fraud Occurs

1. **Call your bank immediately** — request a transaction freeze or chargeback
   (must be done within **24–48 hours** for best results)
2. **Report on cybercrime.gov.in** (National Cybercrime Reporting Portal) —
   or dial **1930** (Cyber Crime Helpline, 24×7)
3. File an **FIR** at the nearest police station (Cyber Crime cell if available)
4. Email your bank's fraud department with the transaction details

## RBI's Zero Liability Rule (Circular 2017)

If the fraud happened due to **bank negligence** (not your fault):
- Report within 3 working days → **full refund** by the bank
- If fraud due to shared OTP / credentials → your liability is limited to ₹5,000–
  ₹25,000 (based on account type and time of reporting)

## If the Fraudster Transferred Money via UPI

- NPCI can freeze the recipient account temporarily if reported quickly
- Contact your UPI app (Google Pay / PhonePe / Paytm) via their dispute portal
  — they can escalate to NPCI within 24 hours

## Prevention Tips

- **Never share OTP, CVV, or UAN** on any call
- Always verify payment received in your bank app, not just the screenshot
- Use a **separate current account** for business payments with daily limit set

## Helplines

- Cyber crime helpline: **1930** (24×7)
- Online reporting: **cybercrime.gov.in**
- RBI banking ombudsman: **14448**
