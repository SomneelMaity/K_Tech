## Who Needs FSSAI Authorisation?

Every **Food Business Operator (FBO)** — anyone who manufactures, processes,
stores, distributes, or sells food — must obtain FSSAI authorisation.

## Three Tiers of FSSAI Authorisation

| Type | Eligibility | Validity | Approx Fee/Year |
|------|-------------|----------|-----------------|
| **Basic Registration** | Annual turnover ≤ ₹12 lakh OR petty retailer / hawker / home baker | 1–5 years | ₹100/year |
| **State Licence** | Turnover ₹12 lakh – ₹20 crore | 1–5 years | ₹2,000–₹5,000/year |
| **Central Licence** | Turnover > ₹20 crore OR operates in 2+ states OR importer | 1–5 years | ₹7,500/year |

## How to Apply (Online)

1. Visit **foscos.fssai.gov.in**
2. Select FBO type → choose Registration or Licence
3. Fill form (business details, address, food category, equipment list)
4. Pay fee online
5. Inspection (for licences) → Licence/Registration issued within **30–60 days**

## Penalties for Operating Without FSSAI Authorisation

- Operating without registration: fine up to **₹5 lakh**
- Selling unsafe food: imprisonment up to 6 months + fine up to ₹1 lakh
- Sub-standard food: fine up to ₹5 lakh

## Display Requirements

- FSSAI registration/licence number must be displayed **prominently** at the
  premises and printed on all food labels/packages

## Renewal

- Renew 30 days before expiry on foscos.fssai.gov.in
- Late renewal: penalty of ₹100/day up to maximum of original fee

## Helplines

- FSSAI helpline: **1800-112-100** (toll-free)
- Portal: **foscos.fssai.gov.in**
