## The 45-Day Rule

Under **MSMED Act 2006 Section 15**, if you are a registered MSME (Udyam
registration), the buyer must pay you within:
- **15 days** if there is no written agreement
- **45 days** if there is a written agreement (maximum allowed)

## Interest on Delayed Payments (Section 16)

If payment is not made within the agreed/statutory period:
- Interest accrues at **3 times the bank rate** notified by the RBI
- Interest is **compounded monthly**
- This right exists even without a written agreement, automatically

**Example:** Invoice ₹1,00,000, overdue 6 months, RBI bank rate 6.5%:
Effective interest rate = 6.5% × 3 = 19.5% p.a., compounded monthly.

## How to File on MSME Samadhaan (Section 18)

1. Register / log in at **samadhaan.msme.gov.in**
2. File an application — attach invoice, delivery proof, payment history
3. The **MSME Facilitation Council** (state-level) will conciliate within 45 days
4. If conciliation fails → automatic reference to arbitration (no court fees)
5. The process is **free** for the MSME applicant

## Pre-requisites

- You must have **Udyam Registration** (register free at udyamregistration.gov.in)
- The buyer must be a company / large buyer (not another MSME for §18 purposes)

## What to do

1. Ensure Udyam registration is active
2. Send a formal payment reminder to the buyer citing MSMED Act §15–16 (keep proof)
3. Wait 15 days for response
4. If unpaid → file on Samadhaan portal
