## When is GST Registration Mandatory?

| Situation | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Goods supply (most states) | Annual turnover > **₹40 lakh** |
| Services supply | Annual turnover > **₹20 lakh** |
| North-eastern / special category states | > **₹10 lakh** (services) |
| E-commerce seller (any turnover) | Mandatory regardless of turnover |
| Inter-state supply of goods | Mandatory regardless of turnover |

## How to Register

1. Visit **gst.gov.in → Register**
2. Submit PAN, Aadhaar, business address, bank account details, and trade licence
3. OTP verification → Application Reference Number (ARN) generated
4. GSTIN (15-digit) issued within **7 working days** (or 3 days for Aadhaar-authenticated)

## Key GST Returns (Regular Taxpayer)

| Return | Frequency | Due Date |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| GSTR-1 (outward supplies) | Monthly / Quarterly | 11th of next month |
| GSTR-3B (summary + tax payment) | Monthly | 20th of next month |
| GSTR-9 (annual) | Annual | 31 December |

## Composition Scheme (Simpler Option)

If turnover ≤ ₹1.5 crore (goods) or ₹50 lakh (services), opt for **Composition Scheme**:
- Pay flat GST rate (1%–6% of turnover)
- File only 1 quarterly return (CMP-08) + 1 annual return (GSTR-4)
- Cannot issue tax invoice or claim input tax credit

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

- Non-registration: 10% of tax due or ₹10,000, whichever is higher
- Late return: ₹50/day (₹20/day for nil returns), capped at ₹5,000
