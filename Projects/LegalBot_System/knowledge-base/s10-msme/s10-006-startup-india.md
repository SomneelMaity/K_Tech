## What is a Startup Under the Policy?

A DPIIT-recognised startup must:
- Be incorporated as a **Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm**
- Be less than **10 years** from incorporation date
- Annual turnover not exceeded **₹100 crore** in any year since incorporation
- Be **working towards innovation**, development, or improvement of products/processes
  (not merely replicating an existing business)

## How to Apply for DPIIT Recognition (Free)

1. Visit **startupindia.gov.in → Get Recognised**
2. Sign up, fill in company details, describe the innovative nature of the business
3. Upload Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, and a brief document explaining the
   innovation
4. Recognition certificate issued in **2–3 working days** (no fee)

## Key Benefits

### Tax Benefits (Section 80-IAC)

- **3-year tax holiday**: Eligible startups can claim **100% tax exemption** on
  profits for any 3 consecutive years out of the first 10 years
- Apply to Inter-Ministerial Board for this certificate

### Angel Tax Exemption (Section 56(2)(viib))

- DPIIT-recognised startups are **exempt from angel tax** — funds raised from
  investors are not taxed as "income from other sources" regardless of valuation

### Other Benefits

- Self-certification compliance: 9 labour laws + 3 environmental laws (no
  inspections for 3–5 years)
- Fast-track patent application: 80% rebate on patent fee, expedited examination
- ₹10,000 rebate on trademark registration
- Wind-up in 90 days under IBC for eligible startups

## Funding Support

- **Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)**: Grants up to ₹20 lakh (proof of
  concept), equity funding up to ₹50 lakh via DPIIT-empanelled incubators
- **Fund of Funds (FFS)**: SIDBI-managed ₹10,000 crore fund investing in VC/AIFs
  that fund startups
