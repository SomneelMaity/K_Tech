## What is CGTMSE?

The **Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE)** is a
scheme run by SIDBI and the Ministry of MSME that allows banks to give
**collateral-free loans to MSMEs** up to a specified limit, with the government
providing the guarantee.

## Who is Eligible?

- **Micro and Small Enterprises** with a valid Udyam Registration
- **New or existing** businesses (including startups, traders, manufacturers,
  service providers)
- Not applicable to: agriculture, educational institutions, self-help groups

## Loan Limits Under CGTMSE

| Category | Maximum Guarantee Cover |
|----------|------------------------|
| Micro enterprises | Up to **₹2 crore** (85–100% guarantee) |
| Small enterprises | Up to **₹5 crore** (75–85% guarantee) |
| Women / SC/ST entrepreneurs | Higher guarantee percentage (85%) |

## How to Apply

1. Ensure you have **Udyam Registration** (udyamregistration.gov.in)
2. Approach any scheduled commercial bank, RRB, or NBFC empanelled with CGTMSE
3. Bank evaluates your business plan and creditworthiness
4. Bank applies for CGTMSE guarantee — **you pay a one-time guarantee fee** of
   approximately 1–1.5% of the loan amount (Annual Service Fee thereafter)
5. Loan sanctioned **without requiring property/collateral** as security

## What Banks Cannot Ask Under This Scheme

- **No collateral** (land, building, machinery) as security for covered amounts
- **No third-party guarantee** for amounts covered under CGTMSE

## Annual Guarantee Fee

- CGTMSE levies an **Annual Guarantee Fee (AGF)** — currently 0.37%–2% p.a.
  depending on loan size and category — usually passed on to the borrower

## If the Bank Refuses

- Banks are not obligated to lend under CGTMSE but cannot arbitrarily refuse
  eligible MSMEs
- File a complaint with the **MSME Grievance portal** at udyamassist.gov.in
- RBI Banking Ombudsman: **14448**
