## What Can Be Registered as a Trademark?

A trademark can be a word, logo, name, slogan, colour combination, shape, or
sound that **distinguishes your goods/services** from others. Examples: brand name,
product name, shop signage.

## Why Register?

- Exclusive nationwide right to use the mark (Section 28)
- Legal basis to stop copycats (infringement suit, Section 29)
- Can license the mark and earn royalties
- TM registration is evidence of ownership in court

## Registration Process

| Step | Timeline |
|------|----------|
| File TM application online at ipindia.gov.in | Day 1 |
| Application acknowledged → use ™ symbol immediately | Instant |
| Examination report issued | ~1 month |
| Advertised in Trade Marks Journal | After examination |
| Opposition period (4 months) | 4 months from advertising |
| Registration certificate issued (® symbol) | ~18–24 months total |

## Fees (Startup / Individual / Small Enterprise)

| Filing Type | Fee |
|-------------|-----|
| Online filing (single class) | **₹4,500** |
| Physical filing | ₹5,000 |
| Each additional class | Same rate |

Startups and individuals get 50% fee concession compared to large companies.

## Class System

Goods and services are divided into 45 classes (Nice Classification). File in the
class most relevant to your business. Filing in multiple classes requires separate
fees per class.

## Common Rejections (Section 9 Absolute Grounds)

- Descriptive marks (e.g., "Best Quality Rice" for a rice brand) — not registrable
- Marks identical or similar to existing registered marks (Section 11)
- Offensive or deceptive marks
