## What is Udyam Registration?

Udyam Registration is the **official MSME registration** issued by the Ministry
of MSME. It replaced Udyog Aadhaar from 1 July 2020. It is **free of charge** and
entirely online.

## MSME Classification (Revised 2020)

| Category | Investment in Plant & Machinery | Annual Turnover |
|----------|--------------------------------|-----------------|
| Micro    | Up to ₹1 crore                | Up to ₹5 crore  |
| Small    | Up to ₹10 crore               | Up to ₹50 crore |
| Medium   | Up to ₹50 crore               | Up to ₹250 crore |

## How to Register (Step by Step)

1. Visit **udyamregistration.gov.in**
2. Click "For New Entrepreneurs who are not Registered yet as MSME"
3. Enter your **Aadhaar number** and validate with OTP
4. Fill in business details (NIC code, address, bank account, PAN)
5. Self-declare investment and turnover
6. Submit — **Udyam Registration Certificate** generated immediately (free PDF)

No documents need to be uploaded; the portal auto-validates with IT/GST databases.

## Key Benefits of Udyam Registration

- **Priority Sector Lending** — banks must lend at lower interest rates
- **45-day payment protection** under MSMED Act §15 (delayed payment interest)
- **Government tender preference** (25% procurement reserved for MSMEs)
- **Credit Guarantee (CGTMSE)** — collateral-free loans up to ₹5 crore
- **Subsidies** on patents, trademarks, and ISO certification
- **Samadhan portal** access for delayed payment recovery
