## Licences Required for a Cloud Kitchen in Maharashtra

### 1. FSSAI Registration / Licence (Mandatory)

| Annual Turnover | Type | Fee (approx.) | Portal |
|----------------|------|---------------|--------|
| Up to ₹12 lakh | Basic Registration | ₹100/year | foscos.fssai.gov.in |
| ₹12 lakh – ₹20 crore | State Licence | ₹2,000–5,000/year | foscos.fssai.gov.in |
| Above ₹20 crore | Central Licence | ₹7,500/year | foscos.fssai.gov.in |

Apply before starting operations. Operating without FSSAI is punishable.

### 2. GST Registration

- Mandatory if annual turnover exceeds **₹20 lakh** (₹10 lakh for North-East/hill states)
- For online food delivery (Swiggy/Zomato), TCS is deducted — GST registration
  advisable even below threshold
- Register at **gst.gov.in** (free)

### 3. Maharashtra Shops & Establishments Registration

- Required if you employ any workers (even domestic help for packaging)
- Register with the local Municipal Corporation / Labour Office
- Fee: nominal (₹500–2,000 depending on number of employees)

### 4. Municipal Trade Licence / Health Licence

- Required from the local Municipal Corporation (BMC in Mumbai)
- For food premises — includes a basic inspection

### 5. Fire NOC (if applicable)

- Required if the premises area exceeds limits set by the fire brigade
- Apply to the local Fire Brigade office

### 6. Udyam Registration (MSME) — Strongly Recommended

- Free at udyamregistration.gov.in
- Unlocks MSME Samadhaan, priority lending, government tenders

### Suggested Order

1. FSSAI registration (start here — takes 7–30 days)
2. GST registration
3. Udyam registration
4. Shops & Establishments registration
5. Municipal trade licence + Fire NOC (if applicable)
