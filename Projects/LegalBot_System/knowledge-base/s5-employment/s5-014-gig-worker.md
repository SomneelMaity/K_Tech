## Who is a Gig Worker?

The Code on Social Security 2020 defines a **gig worker** as someone who performs
work or participates in a work arrangement and earns from such activity outside
of a traditional employer-employee relationship. Examples: Swiggy/Zomato delivery
partners, Ola/Uber drivers, Urban Company service providers, freelancers.

## Current Legal Status (as of 2026)

The Code on Social Security 2020 has been **enacted but not yet fully notified**
for gig workers. The central government is yet to frame the specific rules and
schemes under Section 114. However:

- **Rajasthan** enacted a separate Gig Workers Welfare Act 2023 — platform
  companies must contribute 1–2% of each transaction to a welfare fund
- Other states are drafting similar laws

## What Gig Workers Can Access NOW

### e-Shram Registration

- Register at **eshram.gov.in** or call **14434**
- Provides ₹2 lakh accidental death insurance under PM Suraksha Bima Yojana

### GST Registration

- If annual income exceeds ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in some states), GST registration
  is required even for gig/freelance work

## Disputes with Platforms

Currently gig workers cannot raise industrial disputes (not "workmen"). Options:
1. Consumer Forum if the platform is acting as a service provider to you
2. Civil court for contract disputes
3. Labour Department if the state has a gig-worker-specific law

## What is Pending

The central government is expected to notify gig worker social security schemes
including pension, health insurance, and accident cover linked to transaction fees.
