## Types of PF Withdrawal

| Situation | Form | Waiting Period |
|-----------|------|---------------|
| Full withdrawal after resignation | Form 19 (EPF) + Form 10C (EPS) | 2 months after leaving |
| Partial withdrawal (medical) | Form 31 | After 1 year of membership |
| Partial withdrawal (home purchase) | Form 31 | After 5 years |
| Partial withdrawal (marriage/education) | Form 31 | After 7 years |
| Advance for COVID / natural calamity | Form 31 | Anytime (special cases) |

## How to Withdraw (Online — Fastest)

1. Activate your **UAN** (Universal Account Number) on **unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in**
2. Link Aadhaar, PAN, and bank account to UAN and verify them
3. Go to: Online Services → Claim (Form 31, 19, 10C) → Select claim type
4. Approve via OTP to Aadhaar-linked mobile
5. Amount credited within **7–10 working days**

## Offline Process

- Download the relevant form from epfindia.gov.in
- Get it attested by the employer
- Submit at the nearest **EPFO regional office**

## Partial Withdrawal for Housing (Para 68B)

- Up to 90% of your EPF balance after 5 years of membership
- Can be used to purchase/construct a house or repay a home loan

## TDS on PF Withdrawal

- No TDS if continuous service ≥ 5 years
- If service < 5 years and withdrawal ≥ ₹50,000: TDS at **10%** (submit Form 15G/15H
  to avoid TDS if income is below taxable limit)
