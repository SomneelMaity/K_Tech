## What Counts as Sexual Harassment?

Under the POSH Act, sexual harassment includes:
- Physical contact or advances
- Demand or request for sexual favours
- Sexually coloured remarks, jokes, or comments
- Showing pornography
- Any other unwelcome physical, verbal, or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature

It covers harassment by a **supervisor, colleague, client, or visitor** at the
workplace or any work-related event.

## Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) — Section 4

Every employer with **10 or more employees** must constitute an ICC:
- Presiding officer must be a **senior woman employee**
- Minimum 2 members from employees
- At least 1 external member from an NGO or familiar with POSH issues

## How to File a Complaint (Section 9)

1. Submit a **written complaint** to the ICC within **3 months** of the incident
   (extendable by 3 more months for sufficient cause)
2. Include dates, description, names of witnesses if any
3. If your workplace has no ICC (illegal if 10+ employees) → file with the **Local
   Complaints Committee (LCC)** at the District level

## ICC Process (Sections 11–13)

- ICC must complete inquiry within **60 days**
- Both parties heard; documents submitted
- Recommendations sent to employer within **10 days** of inquiry completion
- Employer must act on recommendations within **60 days**

## Interim Relief Available

You can request ICC to recommend:
- Transfer of the respondent or yourself
- Leave for up to 3 months during inquiry

## If the Employer Has No ICC

- File FIR under IPC Section 354A (sexual harassment) at local police station
- Contact the District LCC through the district administration or **Mahila Helpline 181**
- NALSA: **1516**
