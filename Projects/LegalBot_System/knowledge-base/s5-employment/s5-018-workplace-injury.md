## Who Can Claim?

Any employee (or their dependants) who suffers injury, disablement, or death due
to an accident **arising out of and in the course of employment**.

Applies to workers not covered by ESI (which takes priority if ESI is applicable).

## Types of Compensation (Section 4)

| Type | Amount |
|------|--------|
| **Death** | 50% of monthly wages × relevant factor (age-based), minimum ₹1.20 lakh |
| **Permanent total disablement** | 60% of monthly wages × relevant factor, minimum ₹1.40 lakh |
| **Permanent partial disablement** | Proportionate to loss of earning capacity (Schedule I) |
| **Temporary disablement** | 25% of monthly wages per week during the disability period |

Monthly wages capped at **₹8,000** for calculation purposes (central government
notified ceiling — check latest notification as it is revised).

## Employer's Responsibility

- The employer must pay compensation **within 1 month** of it becoming due
- Failure → penalty: up to 50% of the compensation amount + interest at 12% p.a.

## How to Claim

1. Notify the employer of the accident **as soon as possible** (Section 10 — within
   2 years is the limitation period)
2. If employer refuses or delays → file claim with the **Commissioner for
   Employees' Compensation** (District Labour Court / Labour Commissioner's office)
3. The process is quasi-judicial; you do not need a lawyer
4. Free legal aid: NALSA **1516**

## Commutation of Compensation

A half-monthly payment (temporary disablement) can be converted to a lump sum
by the Commissioner at the request of either party.

## ESI vs Employees' Compensation Act

If ESI is applicable to you, you must claim under ESI — you cannot claim under
the Employees' Compensation Act. ESI is generally more beneficial.
