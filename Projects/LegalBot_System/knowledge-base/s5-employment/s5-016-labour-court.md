## Which Forum to Approach?

| Type of Dispute | Forum |
|----------------|-------|
| Individual dismissal / retrenchment / wages | **Labour Court** (Section 7) |
| Industry-wide dispute, standing orders | **Industrial Tribunal** (Section 7A) |
| Recovery of money due under an award or settlement | **Labour Court** (Section 33C) |
| Unfair labour practice | **Industrial Court** (in Maharashtra) |

## Section 2A — Individual Right to Raise Dispute

Since 2010, an **individual workman** can directly raise a dispute about wrongful
dismissal/discharge without needing a trade union. File with:
- The **Conciliation Officer** (Labour Commissioner's office) first
- If conciliation fails within 45 days → refer to Labour Court

## How to File

1. Go to the **District Labour Commissioner's** (DLC) office — it's free
2. Submit a written statement of the dispute (facts, dates, relief sought)
3. Conciliation officer issues notice to employer and holds meetings
4. If settlement reached → binding settlement recorded under Section 12(3)
5. If no settlement → DLC sends a failure report → dispute referred to Labour Court

## Labour Court Process

- No court fee in labour courts
- You may appear yourself or through a registered trade union
- Legal aid available through DLSA (District Legal Services Authority)
- Labour courts must decide within 3 months for retrenchment/dismissal cases

## Section 33C — Money Recovery

If an award or settlement already exists but the employer hasn't paid:
- File under Section 33C(2) — Labour Court computes and issues a certificate
- Certificate is enforceable like a decree

## Free Legal Aid

- District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) — available in every district
- NALSA helpline: **1516**
