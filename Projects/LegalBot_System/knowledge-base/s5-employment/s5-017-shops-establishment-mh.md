## Applicability

Applies to all shops, commercial establishments, restaurants, hotels, theatres,
IT/BPO companies, and offices in Maharashtra with **10 or more employees**.

## Registration

- Every employer must register on the **Maharashtra S&E portal** within 60 days
  of commencing business (Form A online)
- Certificate valid for **5 years**, renewable
- No registration = fine up to ₹2 lakh

## Working Hours and Rest (Section 8–9)

- Maximum **9 hours/day**, **48 hours/week**
- At least **30 minutes rest** after 5 consecutive hours
- **1 day off per week** (Section 18)

## Leave Entitlements (Section 24)

- **Earned Leave (EL)**: 1 day per 20 days worked (after 3 months of employment)
- **Casual Leave (CL)**: 8 days per year
- **Sick Leave (SL)**: 8 days per year
- Leave can be accumulated up to 45 days (EL)

## Women Employees — Special Protections (Section 36)

- Cannot be required to work between 9:30 PM and 7 AM without consent
- If working late shifts: employer must provide **free transport** and a
  **female escort** (or male security) up to their residence
- Separate rest rooms for women

## Termination and Notice

- One month's notice or wages in lieu for employees with more than 3 months of
  service (both parties)

## Grievances

- File with the **Inspector under the Maharashtra S&E Act** at the District Labour
  Commissioner's office
- NALSA: **1516**
