## Who is Covered?

Every woman employed in any establishment having **10 or more employees** — applies
to factories, mines, plantations, shops, and government offices.

## Maternity Leave Entitlement (Section 5)

| Situation | Paid Leave |
|-----------|-----------|
| First or second child | **26 weeks** (8 weeks before delivery) |
| Third child onwards | **12 weeks** |
| Adoption / surrogacy (child < 3 months) | **12 weeks** |

- Minimum 80 days of employment in the 12 months before the due date required
- Leave can start up to **8 weeks before** expected delivery

## Pay During Maternity Leave

- Full average daily wage for the entire maternity leave period
- Payment must be made **in advance** for the pre-delivery period (on request)

## Additional Rights

- **Nursing breaks**: Two 15-minute breaks per day until the child is 15 months old
- **Crèche facility**: Mandatory for establishments with 50+ employees (Section 11A)
- **Work from home**: Employer may allow WFH after maternity leave if the nature of
  work permits (2017 amendment)

## Protection Against Dismissal (Section 12)

- Dismissing or giving notice of dismissal during maternity leave is **illegal**
- Any such discharge is void and the woman retains all benefits

## What to Do if Rights are Denied

1. File a complaint with the **Inspector under the Maternity Benefit Act** (District
   Labour Commissioner's office)
2. Employer can be fined and imprisoned under Section 21
3. Helpline: iShram portal **14434**, NALSA **1516**
