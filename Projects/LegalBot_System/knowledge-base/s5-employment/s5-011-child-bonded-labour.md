## Child Labour — What is Prohibited?

**Complete ban** on employing children below **14 years** in any occupation or
process (Section 3, 2016 Amendment).

Children aged **14–18 years (adolescents)** cannot work in **hazardous occupations**
(mines, explosives, construction involving cranes, etc.).

Domestic work, family businesses, and entertainment are now also regulated.

## Penalties for Employing Child Labour

- First offence: imprisonment 6 months to 2 years, or fine ₹20,000–₹50,000, or both
- Subsequent offence: imprisonment 1–3 years (mandatory)
- Parents/guardians who allow it: fine up to ₹10,000

## Bonded Labour — Zero Tolerance

The Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act 1976 **abolishes all bonded labour**. Any
agreement to work in exchange for a loan/advance that restricts freedom is void
from the date of the Act.

## Immediate Relief for Bonded Labourers

- District Magistrate must release bonded labourer **immediately** on complaint
  (Section 12)
- State government must provide rehabilitation (land, housing, credit)
- Interim relief: ₹20,000 per victim (central scheme)

## How to Report Child or Bonded Labour

1. Call **CHILDLINE: 1098** (free, 24×7) — for children in distress
2. File FIR at the nearest police station under the CLPRA / Bonded Labour Act
3. Complain to the **District Magistrate** who has suo-motu powers
4. Labour Commissioner's office can also receive complaints
