## Who is a Domestic Worker?

A person employed in a household for cleaning, cooking, childcare, eldercare,
gardening, driving, or similar work. Domestic workers are part of the "unorganised
sector" and have limited but growing statutory protections.

## Key Protections Available

### Minimum Wages

Several states (Delhi, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala) have notified
**minimum wages for domestic workers**. Check your state Labour Department website.
The employer must pay at least the notified rate.

### e-Shram Registration (Free)

- Register on **eshram.gov.in** or call **14434**
- Get a free Universal Account Number (UAN) and e-Shram card
- Provides access to PM Suraksha Bima Yojana: ₹2 lakh accidental death/disability
  insurance for ₹12/year premium (paid by government for unorganised workers)

### ESI Coverage (Some States)

Maharashtra and a few other states have extended ESI (Employee State Insurance)
to domestic workers — check with local ESIC office.

## If a Domestic Worker is Harassed or Assaulted

- File FIR at the nearest police station
- For sexual harassment: Mahila helpline **181**
- CHILDLINE **1098** if the worker is a minor (illegal to employ children under 14)

## State-Specific: Maharashtra

Maharashtra has a **Domestic Workers Welfare Board** — domestic workers can
register and access welfare benefits including accident insurance.

## Steps for Employers

- Issue a simple written appointment letter noting wages, leave, and duties
- Contribute to PF/ESI if employing on a regular basis with sufficient wages

## Helplines

- e-Shram helpline: **14434**
- NALSA: **1516**
- iShram: **14434**
