## Standard Working Hours (Factories Act 1948)

- Maximum **9 hours per day** and **48 hours per week** (Section 51, 54)
- Minimum **30-minute rest interval** after 5 consecutive hours of work (Section 55)
- **1 day off per week** — typically Sunday but can be substituted (Section 52)
- Spread of working hours (start to finish) must not exceed **10.5 hours** per day

## Night Shifts

- Women cannot be required to work between **7 PM and 6 AM** without consent and
  safety measures (varies by state amendment)

## Overtime (Section 59)

- Any work beyond 9 hours/day or 48 hours/week → **double the ordinary wage rate**
- Overtime must be paid with regular salary for that period
- Total hours including overtime: maximum **60 hours/week** and **50 hours in any
  quarter** (quarterly cap rarely enforced but legally valid)

## Shops & Commercial Establishments

- Governed by state Shops & Establishments Acts (not Factories Act)
- Limits similar: typically 8–9 hours/day, 48 hours/week, overtime at double rate

## What to Do if Overtime is Not Paid

1. Document the extra hours worked (screenshots, email records, attendance system)
2. Send written demand to employer
3. File complaint with the **Inspector of Factories** (for factory workers) or
   **Labour Inspector** (for shops/offices)
4. The Inspector can impose penalties on the employer
5. NALSA helpline: **1516**

## Note for IT/BPO Employees

Software and IT employees are often excluded from Factories Act but covered by
state-specific IT/ITeS policies. Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu have
separate notifications for IT workers.
