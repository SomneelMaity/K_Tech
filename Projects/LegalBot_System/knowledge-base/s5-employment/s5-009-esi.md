## Who is Covered?

Employees earning up to **₹21,000/month** (₹25,000 for persons with disability)
in factories and establishments with **10 or more employees** (varies by state).

## ESI Contribution Rates

| Contributor | Rate of Wages |
|-------------|--------------|
| Employee    | 0.75%        |
| Employer    | 3.25%        |

Employer must register the employee with ESIC and deposit contributions monthly.

## Benefits Under ESI (Section 46)

| Benefit | Entitlement |
|---------|-------------|
| **Sickness benefit** | 70% of wages for up to 91 days/year |
| **Extended sickness** | 80% for up to 2 years (34 specified diseases) |
| **Maternity benefit** | 100% wages for 26 weeks |
| **Disablement benefit** | 90% wages (temporary); 90% permanently for permanent disability |
| **Dependants' benefit** | 90% of wages split among dependants on death of insured person |
| **Medical benefit** | Full OPD, IPD, surgery for employee + family from day 1 |

## How to Claim

1. Get your **ESIC IP number** (Insured Person number) from HR or ESIC portal
2. Visit the designated ESIC dispensary/hospital for medical treatment
3. For cash benefits (sickness, maternity): submit claim form at your ESIC branch
   office with attendance/salary proof

## If Employer is Not Registering You

- File a complaint at the ESIC Regional/Sub-Regional Office
- Employer faces penalty + back contribution + interest (Section 85)
- ESIC helpline: **1800-11-2526** (toll-free)
