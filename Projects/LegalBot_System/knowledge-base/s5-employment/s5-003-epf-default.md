## Your PF Entitlement

If you earn up to ₹15,000/month basic pay in an establishment with 20+ employees,
EPF deduction is **mandatory** — 12% of basic wages from your salary and a
matching 12% from your employer (3.67% to EPF, 8.33% to EPS).

Establishments with fewer than 20 employees can also voluntarily register.

## How to check if PF is being deposited

1. Activate your **UAN** (Universal Account Number) at unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in
2. Check your **PF passbook** — it shows contributions month-by-month
3. Cross-check against your salary slips (PF deduction column)

If salary slips show PF deducted but the passbook shows no credit → your
employer has **deducted but not deposited** — this is a criminal offence.

## What to do

1. **Raise it with HR** in writing — give 7 days to respond
2. **File a complaint** at **epfigms.gov.in** (EPFO Grievance Portal)
   - Attach: UAN, salary slips, PF passbook screenshots
3. The Regional PF Commissioner can investigate and prosecute the employer
4. Employer is liable for: full dues + penal interest + prosecution under
   Section 14 / 14AA (imprisonment up to 3 years + fine for deliberate default)
