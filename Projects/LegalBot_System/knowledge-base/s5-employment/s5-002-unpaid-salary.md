## The Right to Timely Wages

Under the **Code on Wages 2019**, your employer must pay your wages:
- By the **7th of the following month** (factories, mines, plantations)
- By the **10th of the following month** (all other establishments)

## What counts as wages?

Basic pay, dearness allowance, and any other fixed monetary component. Bonus,
HRA, and other allowances may be separate — check your offer letter.

## What to do if salary is not paid?

1. **Speak to HR first** — put the request in writing (email is fine) and keep a copy
2. If no response in 7 working days, **file a complaint** with the:
   - **Payment of Wages Authority** (usually the Labour Commissioner in your district)
   - **Labour Commissioner's Office** (for conciliation)
3. **Documents to bring:**
   - Offer letter / appointment letter
   - Salary slips (if available)
   - Bank statements showing salary credits / absence of credits
   - Any written communication with HR
4. The process is **free**; no lawyer required for conciliation
5. The Authority can order payment of **all dues plus 10× the unpaid amount** as
   compensation for deliberate withholding (Section 20, Payment of Wages Act 1936,
   which continues to apply for enforcement)

## For gig / platform workers

The Code on Wages covers gig workers if the platform qualifies as an
"establishment." Enforcement is evolving — file a complaint and cite the Code.
