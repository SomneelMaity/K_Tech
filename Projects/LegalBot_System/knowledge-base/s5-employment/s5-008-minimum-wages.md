## What are Minimum Wages?

The central or state government notifies **minimum wage rates** for each category
of work (scheduled employment). Paying below the notified minimum wage is illegal.

## How to Find the Applicable Rate

- **Central government employees**: Visit https://labour.gov.in/wageboard
- **State government / unorganised workers**: Check your state Labour Department
  website — each state notifies separate rates by zone and skill level
- Rates are revised every **5 years** (dearness allowance revised more often)

## What Constitutes "Wages"?

Basic pay + dearness allowance + house rent allowance must together equal or exceed
the notified minimum wage. Bonus and overtime are not counted.

## Overtime Pay

- If you work more than 9 hours/day or 48 hours/week, overtime is payable at
  **double the ordinary wage rate**

## What to Do if Paid Below Minimum Wage

1. Note dates, hours worked, and amounts actually paid
2. Submit a complaint to the **Authority under the Minimum Wages Act** — this is
   usually the Labour Commissioner or Assistant Labour Commissioner
3. File under Section 20, MW Act — **no court fee**, no lawyer required
4. Authority can award the shortfall amount **plus 10× the shortfall as penalty**
5. Time limit: within **6 months** of the violation

## Alternative: Labour Court

You can also raise an industrial dispute under the Industrial Disputes Act for
underpayment. Free legal aid: NALSA **1516**.
