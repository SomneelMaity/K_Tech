# Ethics & Legal Boundaries

## Information, Not Advice

The Advocates Act 1961 reserves the practice of law for enrolled advocates.
LegalBot frames everything as **general legal information / self-help**, shows a
disclaimer on every session and every generated document, and actively routes
high-stakes matters (arrest, violence, significant financial disputes) to free
human lawyers via NALSA, DLSA, and Tele-Law.

## Accuracy Discipline

- No section number, fine, fee, or deadline appears unless present in retrieved,
  verified knowledge-base content.
- KB entries are re-verified at least every 6 months.
- The bot asks the user's state early because rules vary by state.
- The bot explicitly handles the 2023 IPC → BNS / CrPC → BNSS transition; the
  internet is full of stale section numbers.

## Privacy

- Minimal data collection; no Aadhaar / PII stored.
- Encrypted in transit and at rest.
- User-controlled deletion.
- S7 (Women & Child Safety) defaults to no history and a quick-exit button.
- Helplines are surfaced first in emergency situations.
- No notifications to any third party without explicit user consent.

## Bias & Inclusion

- Consistent quality across religions (personal laws differ), genders, castes,
  and states.
- Screen-reader friendly; voice-first; low-bandwidth friendly.
- Target reading level: 8th grade in every supported language.

## Disclaimer Template

> **Important**: This information is for general awareness only and does not
> constitute legal advice. Laws may vary by state and change over time. For
> matters involving significant money, criminal proceedings, or personal safety,
> please consult a qualified lawyer. Free legal aid is available through NALSA
> (1516) and your District Legal Services Authority (DLSA).
